Behind the Lens

I’m a professional photographer driven by one thing — telling real stories through honest, intentional imagery. What started as a passion quickly became a career built on trust, consistency, and emotion.

With years of experience shooting weddings, portraits, events, and commercial projects, I focus on capturing moments as they naturally unfold — not forced, not staged, just real.

My Style Philosophy

  • Cinematic: Dramatic lighting, depth, and mood that feels timeless.
  • Documentary: Real moments captured as they happen, without interruption.
  • Editorial: Clean, intentional compositions with a refined finish.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about sessions, delivery, and payment.

What is your typical turnaround time for edited photos?

For standard sessions, edited photos are delivered within 7–10 business days. Weddings or large events may take 2–3 weeks.

In what format are the final images delivered?

Images are delivered digitally via secure download links in high-resolution JPEG format. Print-ready files are included.

How do i book a session?

Simply fill out the booking form, select your service, and we’ll confirm availability. A 30% deposit secures your date.

What are your payment terms?

A 30% deposit is required to secure your booking. The remaining balance is due upon delivery of final images.
