Harnessing

Good lighting beats expensive gear. Mobile sensors have limited dynamic range—facing natural light ensures your signal-to-noise ratio remains optimal. Avoid harsh midday exposure to prevent highlight clipping.

02

Manual
Exposure Logic

Don't trust the auto-exposure. Tap-to-focus then slide the exposure down manually. Slightly underexposed frames preserve the data in your highlights, allowing for professional-grade grading in post.

03

The "Burst"
Workflow

Mobile photography is about agility. Use the rule of thirds to anchor your subject, and always "zoom with your feet." Digital zoom is just image degradation.
